# Data Governance

Sources are public/open, licensed commercial, user-authorized, or synthetic/test. Every observation records source identity, source record ID, observed/acquired time, geometry/CRS, license/terms, ingestion time, parser/schema version, raw payload hash, and quality/freshness metadata.

Retention and redistribution follow source terms. Derived facts retain their evidence chain.

Prohibited: covert collection, unauthorized access, stalking, facial recognition, named-person surveillance, credential harvesting, and access-control bypass.
